How to install on your iPhone SE

  1. Open this website on your iPhone SE.
  2. Tap Download AiboOS IPA and save it to Files.
  3. Open TrollStore, tap +, then select the downloaded IPA.
  4. Tap Install and look for AiboOS on your Home Screen.

Experimental app beta. This runs inside iOS and does not replace iOS. Android APK apps do not run in this build. If installation fails, please share the exact TrollStore error.
