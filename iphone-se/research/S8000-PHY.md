# S8000 PHY driver track (n69u)
Status: research started; no runtime driver binding or hardware writes.

## Evidence
Pinned OrchardLinux kernel ef92d0d3b34aecba1fca233c4b983f89d1dbaaa1, drivers/pci/controller/pcie-apple-h9p.c:
- apple_s8003_setup_common programs RC lane configuration and applies PHY/RC tunables.
- apple_s8003_start_link calls apple_s8003_program_phy_lanes, which accesses base_phy_ip directly.
- The S8000 public ADT has no matching separate PHY-IP window; merely making its mapping optional is insufficient.
- Existing independent PHY resource patch is a prerequisite only.
- Port 1 hosts BCM4345 on n69u. No S8003 compatible fallback.

## Research inputs
The public restore metadata extractor now allows named PCIe tunable properties, preserving opaque raw bytes with existing bounds and pinned firmware hashes. A missing property is not evidence that a tuning sequence is unnecessary. Do not interpret or apply unknown records.
Reference: https://asahilinux.org/docs/hw/soc/apcie/

## Implementation gates
1. Audit actual S8000 and S8003 tunable presence and format against pinned source and published hardware evidence.
2. Establish S8000-specific lane layout, PHY initialization, clock/reset and power prerequisites; document sources and unknowns.
3. Add separate S8000 operations with bounded waits, cleanup/error paths and no PHY-IP accesses unless a resource is independently verified.
4. Compile against pinned kernel and validate inactive board DT. Keep controller disabled until a reviewed test plan exists.
5. First physical test: RAM-only, Wi-Fi port only, no storage activation; check thermals, enumerate PCI and collect logs. Stop on abnormal heat.
6. Only after enumeration, validate DART mapping and matching Broadcom firmware/modules.

Do not claim functional PHY or Wi-Fi from source or compile tests. No hardware access, no iOS changes, no new physical boot is required for this research.
